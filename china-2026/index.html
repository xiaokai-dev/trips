The Complete Itinerary

Every landmark, every meal, every magical moment — carefully planned for the ultimate winter experience.

Day 01 Saturday, December 19

✈️ Departure & Transit to Beijing

16:10 – 20:05 Fly SGN → Guangzhou (CAN) on China Southern CZ374
22:00 – 00:55 Connect CAN → Beijing Daxing (PKX) on CZ3165. Arrive at 00:55 AM.
📍 View Beijing Daxing Airport on Google Maps
Day 02 Sunday, December 20

🏯 Beijing's Imperial Central Axis

11:30 – 12:30 Tiananmen Square — Free, but reserve 1–7 days ahead (WeChat, by passport). Pass security with the same passport, walk north toward the Meridian Gate.
12:30 – 16:00 The Forbidden City (Palace Museum) — Walk the central imperial axis, explore the Treasure Gallery. Last entry 15:30.
16:15 – 17:00 Jingshan Park — Climb to Wanchun Pavilion for a panoramic sunset (~16:50) over the golden roofs.
18:00 – 21:00 Wangfujing Avenue — Copper-pot lamb hotpot dinner & stroll the pedestrian shopping avenue.
📍 Open in Google Maps
Day 03 Monday, December 21

🧱 The Great Wall & Lakeside Hutongs

08:30 – 15:30 Mutianyu Great Wall — Cable car up to Watchtower 14, walk to Watchtower 20 (highest point), toboggan slide down! Toboggan doesn't run in snow/rain — chairlift instead.
16:30 – 20:30 OptionalShichahai Lakes & Hutongs — Yandai Xiejie, lakeside bars, dinner in historic courtyard alleys. Ice rinks usually open late Dec–mid Jan, so no skating yet.
📍 Open in Google Maps
Day 04 Tuesday, December 22

🏔️ Imperial Gardens & Southern Heritage

09:00 – 12:30 Summer Palace — Longevity Hill, the 728m painted Long Corridor, frozen Kunming Lake.
14:00 – 16:00 Temple of Heaven — Hall of Prayer for Good Harvests, Echo Wall, Circular Mound Altar. Halls last entry 16:30.
17:00 – 21:00 Qianmen Street & Dashilar — ~2 km NW (walk or Metro Line 8). Street snacks, legendary Peking Duck dinner.
📍 Open in Google Maps
Day 05 Wednesday, December 23

🚄 Bullet Train to Harbin & Russian Downtown

08:00 – 13:00 High-speed train Beijing Chaoyang → Harbin West — ~4.5–5.5 h bullet train (e.g. G901), then 30–40 min to Daoli District.
15:30 – 16:30 Saint Sophia's Church — 1907 Neo-Byzantine cathedral, red brick glowing at dusk.
17:30 – 21:00 Zhongyang Street & Songhua River — Madieer popsicle in -20°C, Russian dinner, frozen river walk.
📍 Open in Google Maps
Day 06 Thursday, December 24

🏰 Tigers & Ice World Magic

09:00 – 11:00 Siberian Tiger Park — Caged safari bus through snowy habitats with Amur tigers. Taxi ~25 min from Daoli.
11:30 – 13:30 OptionalSun Island Snow Sculpture Expo — Only if open (opened Dec 27 in 2024 & 2025). Otherwise: long hot lunch, head to Ice World early.
14:00 – 20:00 Harbin Ice & Snow World — Crystal ice castles, new Frozen-themed zone, Super Ice Slide, Ferris Wheel, lights on at dusk! 2026 opening date TBA (2025: Dec 17).
📍 Open in Google Maps
Day 07 Friday, December 25

🎄 Russian Estate & Flight to Shanghai

09:00 – 13:00 OptionalVolga Manor — Snow-covered Russian estate, St. Nicholas replicas, birch forests & snow tubing. 45–60 min each way, leave by 13:00.
14:00 – 17:30 Laodaowai Baroque Quarter — European facades hiding Chinese courtyards, pork dumplings & iron-pot stew.
21:55 – 00:55 Fly Harbin (HRB) → Shanghai Pudong (PVG) on Spring Airlines 9C6190.
📍 View Volga Manor on Google Maps
Day 08 Saturday, December 26

🌃 Nanjing Road, Lujiazui & The Bund

11:30 – 14:00 People's Square & Nanjing Road — Shengjianbao & xiaolongbao for lunch, stroll east toward the river.
14:30 – 17:30 Lujiazui Financial District — Oriental Pearl Tower, Shanghai Tower 118th-floor observation deck, skybridge walk.
17:45 – 21:00 2 RMB Ferry & The Bund — Cross Huangpu River at sunset, walk the legendary Bund promenade as the skyline blazes.
📍 Open in Google Maps
Day 09 Sunday, December 27

🏮 Yu Garden & French Concession

09:00 – 12:00 Classical Yu Garden & Bazaar — Ming Dynasty garden, Zig-Zag Bridge, Yuyuan Old Street treasures. Closed Mondays; last entry 16:00.
12:30 – 14:30 Xintiandi → Tianzifang — Restored Shikumen lanes and alley shops, 15 min walk apart (Metro Line 10 from Yu Garden).
15:00 – 17:00 Anfu Road → Wukang Road — Plane-tree streets of the Former French Concession, Wukang Mansion photo stop.
17:30 – 21:00 OptionalJing'an Temple & Nanjing West — Metro Line 7 from Changshu Rd. Golden temple exterior at night, Starbucks Reserve Roastery.

💻 Remote Work & Evening Unwind

All Day Indoors / Remote Work — Order warm local lunch via Meituan or Ele.me (Alipay mini-app).
18:15 – 21:30 Evening dinner & packing — Crab roe noodles, Haidilao Hotpot, or optional Huangpu River night cruise.

🛬 Departure via Hong Kong → Home

09:30 – 12:40 Fly Shanghai (PVG) → Hong Kong (HKG) on Cathay Pacific CX365.
12:40 – 16:00 HKG Layover — Roast goose & dim sum at the airport, walk the SkyBridge.

  • Prepay hotels & the train on Trip.com in USD with your card — no Alipay fee on ~¥6,000 of spending.
  • Book ticketed sights on Trip.com too: Ice & Snow World, Mutianyu, Shanghai Tower.
  • Keep Alipay for everyday spending under ¥200: meals, metro, DiDi, small tickets.
  • Ask to split large bills — e.g. a ¥300 dinner into 2 × ¥150.
  • Ask your bank what foreign-transaction fee it charges. At 0%, your real rate is closer to ¥6.5.
  • Keep ~¥500 in cash as a backup — a Bank of China or ICBC ATM at the airport beats changing money in Phnom Penh.
Do the first two and the mid-range spending in China comes to ≈ US$1,880 (≈ US$2,420 for the whole trip).

✂️ Ways to Save ¥2,000–3,000

🏨 Hotels
  • Budget hotels (¥250–350/night) in Beijing & Shanghai, but keep a good-location hotel in Harbin, where the cold makes location and heating matter.
  • Book Harbin now with free cancellation, then check the price again in November. Christmas week and the ice festival push rates up.
  • Skip the hotel breakfast (¥60–120). Jianbing, baozi or soy milk costs ¥8–20, which saves ~¥400–800 over the trip.
  • Use the kettle in your room. Hot water is free everywhere, so fill a thermos instead of buying drinks at sights.
🚇 Getting Around
  • Bus package to Mutianyu instead of a private car — saves ~¥500.
  • Metro inside cities (¥3–6) instead of DiDi (¥30–60). Save DiDi for late nights, Harbin's Songbei District and luggage.
  • Maglev on Day 11 (¥50 + a short taxi) instead of a taxi all the way to the airport.
  • Use DiDi at airports, not the drivers who approach you in arrivals. Their offers usually cost 2–3× more.
  • Pre-buy your Spring Airlines bag online. Paying at the airport counter costs much more.
🎫 Sights
  • Skip the Shanghai Tower deck (¥180). The skybridge and Bund lights are free, or have a ¥40–50 coffee with the view at Duoyun Books on the 52nd floor.
  • Skip the Snow Expo & Volga Manor — ~¥300 in tickets + ~¥200 in taxis.
  • Jingshan Park (a few yuan) is Beijing's best view. You don't need a paid tower.
  • Shanghai Museum at People's Square is free with an online reservation, good for very cold or rainy hours.
  • Buy combo tickets on Trip.com (e.g. Mutianyu entry + shuttle + lift).
🍜 Food
  • Order a half duck at Siji Minfu if there are only one or two of you.
  • Have the big meal at lunch. Lunch sets (套餐) are cheaper for the same dishes.
  • Group-buy deals (团购) on Meituan or Dianping often take 20–40% off restaurant meals and hotpot.
  • Snack away from the sights. Food inside Ice & Snow World, Mutianyu and Yu Garden Bazaar costs 2–3× more.
🛍️ Shopping & Money
  • Buy heat packs (暖宝宝) in China at supermarkets or pharmacies, ~¥1–3 each.
  • Buy souvenirs away from the sights. Chocolates on Zhongyang St and "antiques" at Yu Garden are marked up.
  • Use an eSIM data plan (~US$10–15 for 10 GB) instead of roaming.
  • Follow the 💳 fee tips above: prepay on Trip.com, and keep Alipay payments under ¥200.

Essentials

Travel Tips & Prep

Key things to know before your winter China adventure.

📱

Essential Apps

Alipay & WeChat — Link your Visa/Mastercard for cashless payments, Metro QR codes, and DiDi ride-hailing. Install an eSIM (Airalo/Nomad) + China VPN before departure.

🧥

Winter Packing

Harbin: -22°C! Bring heavy thermals, insulated boots, ski gloves, balaclava & adhesive heat packs (nuanbaobao). Shanghai is milder at 4–10°C.

🎫

Book Ahead

Forbidden City opens 7 days prior at 20:00. Tiananmen Square reservation 1–7 days prior (free, WeChat). Beijing→Harbin train opens 15 days prior. Yu Garden for Sunday. Confirm Ice & Snow World & Snow Expo opening dates in early December.

🪪

Important Documents

✅ Visa obtained. Carry your physical passport everywhere — required for Tiananmen Square security, train boarding, hotel check-in, and attraction entry.

💰

Money & Payments

China is nearly 100% cashless. Alipay accepts international cards. Keep a small amount of cash as backup for rural vendors.

🚄

Getting Around

Use the metro QR code in Alipay for subways. DiDi (in WeChat/Alipay) for taxis. High-speed trains bookable on Trip.com or 12306 app.
