### ✂️ Ways to Save ¥2,000–3,000

**🏨 Hotels**
* Pick **budget hotels** (¥250–350/night) in Beijing and Shanghai, but keep a good-location hotel in Harbin, where the cold makes location and heating matter.
* **Book Harbin now with free cancellation**, then check the price again in November. Christmas week and the ice festival push rates up the closer you get.
* **Skip the hotel breakfast** (often ¥60–120). A local breakfast of jianbing, baozi or soy milk costs ¥8–20 (saves ~¥400–800 over 10 days).
* **Use the kettle in your room.** Hot water is free everywhere in China. Fill a thermos instead of buying drinks at sights (¥5–10 vs ¥2–3 at a convenience store).

**🚇 Getting Around**
* Take the **bus package to Mutianyu** instead of a private car (saves ~¥500).
* **Use the metro inside cities** (¥3–6 a ride) instead of DiDi (¥30–60). Save DiDi for late nights, Harbin's Songbei District, and when you're carrying luggage.
* Take the **Maglev** (¥50 + a short taxi) on Day 11 instead of a taxi all the way to the airport.
* **Use DiDi at airports, not the drivers who approach you in arrivals.** DiDi shows the price up front, and the "taxi" offers inside the terminal usually cost 2–3× more.
* **Pre-buy your Spring Airlines checked bag online.** Paying at the airport counter costs much more.

**🎫 Sights**
* Skip the **Shanghai Tower deck** (¥180). The Lujiazui skybridge and Bund lights are free, or have a ¥40–50 coffee with the view at Duoyun Books on the 52nd floor.
* Skip the **Snow Expo and Volga Manor** (~¥300 in tickets + ~¥200 in taxis).
* **Jingshan Park (a few yuan) is Beijing's best view.** You don't need a paid observation tower.
* **Free indoor options for very cold or rainy hours:** the **Shanghai Museum** at People's Square is free with an online reservation (book ahead). Most streets and districts in this plan are free too (see the Tickets section).
* **Buy combo tickets on Trip.com** (e.g., Mutianyu entry + shuttle + lift) instead of separate tickets at the gate.

**🍜 Food**
* **Order a half duck** at Siji Minfu if you're eating alone or with one other person. Most duck restaurants sell halves.
* **Have the big meal at lunch.** Many restaurants have cheaper lunch sets (套餐) for the same dishes.
* **Look for group-buy deals (团购) on Meituan or Dianping** inside Alipay, if the app works for you. They often take 20–40% off restaurant meals and hotpot.
* **Snack instead of eating at the sights.** Food inside Ice & Snow World, Mutianyu and Yu Garden Bazaar costs 2–3× more than a few streets away.

**🛍️ Shopping & Money**
* **Buy heat packs (暖宝宝) at supermarkets or pharmacies in China**, ~¥1–3 each. Don't pack a whole box from home.
* **Buy souvenirs away from the sights.** Russian chocolates on Zhongyang Street and "antiques" at Yu Garden are marked up. Supermarkets and side streets are cheaper.
* **Use an eSIM data plan** (~US$10–15 for 10 GB) instead of your Cambodian roaming.
* Follow the **💳 fee tips** above: prepay hotels and the train on Trip.com, and keep Alipay payments under ¥200.

## 🗓️ Detailed Day-by-Day Plan

### Day 1: Saturday, December 19 – Departure & Transit to Beijing
* **16:10 – 20:05:** Fly from Ho Chi Minh City (SGN) to Guangzhou Baiyun International Airport (CAN) on **China Southern CZ374**.
* **20:05 – 22:00 (1 hr 55 min Layover):** Clear Chinese immigration at Guangzhou (CAN), then go to the domestic transfer gate. China Southern's minimum connection time is 60 min. On a single ticket your bag is checked through: **at SGN, check that the bag tag says PKX**, and after immigration show your boarding pass and bag tag to the transfer staff.
* **22:00 – 00:55 (+1):** Fly from Guangzhou (CAN) to Beijing Daxing International Airport (PKX) on **China Southern CZ3165**.
* **Overnight Strategy (00:55 Arrival on Dec 20):**
  * *Option A (Downtown Hotel, recommended):* Book your downtown Beijing hotel starting **Dec 19** (4 nights: Dec 19–23) and message the front desk to hold your room for a ~02:30 AM check-in. The airport express train has stopped by then, so take a DiDi or metered taxi (~60 mins, ~¥200+ at night).
  * *Option B (Airport Hotel/Pod):* Stay at a nearby Daxing airport hotel ($25–$40 with free 24h shuttle) or terminal sleep pod ($30–$55), then take the Daxing Airport Express train into the city on Sunday morning and drop your bags at your downtown hotel (booked for 3 nights: Dec 20–23).

### Day 2: Sunday, December 20 – Beijing's Imperial Central Axis
* *Why today:* The Forbidden City is **closed on Mondays**, so Sunday is your essential day to visit.
* **Morning:** Sleep in and recover from your late-night arrival.
* **11:30 AM – 12:30 PM | Tiananmen Square:** **Reservation required** (free, made 1–7 days ahead with your passport via WeChat). Bring the same physical passport through security and walk north toward the Meridian Gate.
* **12:30 PM – 4:00 PM | The Forbidden City (Palace Museum):** Walk the central imperial axis and explore the Treasure Gallery (winter hours: 08:30 – 16:30, last entry 15:30). Exit through the North Gate (Shenwumen).
* **4:15 PM – 5:00 PM | Jingshan Park:** Cross the street directly opposite the Forbidden City's north gate and climb 10 minutes up to Wanchun Pavilion for a panoramic sunset (~16:50) over the golden palace roofs.
* **6:00 PM – 9:00 PM | Wangfujing Avenue:** Head east to Wangfujing for a warm dinner (traditional copper-pot lamb hotpot; Peking Duck is saved for Day 4) and stroll along the pedestrian shopping avenue.

---

### Day 3: Monday, December 21 – The Great Wall & Northern Lakeside Hutongs
* *Why today:* The Forbidden City and the Temple of Heaven halls are closed on Mondays, so it's the best day for the Great Wall (open daily).
* **8:30 AM – 3:30 PM | Mutianyu Great Wall:** Take a private DiDi or a Trip.com ticket-plus-bus package north (~1.5 hours). Winter hours: 08:00 – 17:30. Ride the enclosed cable car up to Watchtower 14, walk the scenic wall toward Watchtower 20 (the highest point) and down to Watchtower 6, then take the toboggan or chairlift down. *(The toboggan does not run on snowy or rainy days.)*
* **4:30 PM – 8:30 PM | Shichahai Lakes & Hutongs:** Stop at Shichahai (Qianhai and Houhai Lakes) on the north side of central Beijing. Stroll through Yandai Xiejie (Skewed Tobacco Pouch Street), browse the lakeside bars, and have dinner in the historic courtyard alleys. *(The lake ice rinks usually open late Dec to mid-Jan, so don't expect skating on Dec 21.)*

---

### Day 4: Tuesday, December 22 – Northwest Imperial Gardens & Southern Heritage
* **9:00 AM – 12:30 PM | Summer Palace:** Take Metro Line 4 to Beigongmen or Xiyuan Station. Explore Longevity Hill, the 728-meter painted Long Corridor, and the frozen expanse of Kunming Lake. *(If you want a slower day, this is the first thing to drop.)*
* **12:30 PM – 2:00 PM | Lunch & transfer:** ~60 min by metro (Line 4 ➔ Line 2 ➔ Line 5) to Tiantan Dongmen (East Gate) Station.
* **2:00 PM – 4:00 PM | Temple of Heaven:** Walk from the Hall of Prayer for Good Harvests along the Danbi Bridge to the Echo Wall and Circular Mound Altar *(halls 08:00 – 17:00, last entry 16:30)*.
* **5:00 PM – 9:00 PM | Qianmen Street & Dashilar:** ~2 km northwest of the Temple of Heaven (25-minute walk or Metro Line 8). Walk the historic pedestrian avenue with its traditional archways, street snacks, and time-honored restaurants. **Peking Duck dinner tonight.**

---

### Day 5: Wednesday, December 23 – High-Speed Train to Harbin & Russian Downtown
* **Morning (~8:00 AM – 1:00 PM) | Bullet Train to Harbin:** Board a morning G-train from **Beijing Chaoyang Station** (e.g., G901 at ~08:00) to **Harbin West Station** (~4.5 to 5.5 hours). Confirm the train number and times on 12306 when booking.
* **Afternoon (1:30 PM – 3:30 PM) | Hotel Check-In & Gear Up:** Harbin West ➔ Daoli District is ~30–40 min by taxi or metro. Check into your hotel near Zhongyang Street and put on your heavy thermal layers, boots, and adhesive heat packs (*nuanbaobao*).
* **3:30 PM – 4:30 PM | Saint Sophia's Church:** Arrive just before dusk (~15:50 sunset) to photograph the 1907 red-brick Neo-Byzantine cathedral in daylight and watch the plaza lights turn on.

### Day 6: Thursday, December 24 – Songbei District (Tigers & Ice World)

* **2:00 PM – 8:00 PM | Harbin Ice & Snow World:** ~10–15 min by taxi from the Tiger Park or Sun Island. Arrive in daylight to see the crystal-clear ice castles, immediately scan the park's WeChat QR code to reserve your virtual queue for the Super Ice Slide and Snowflake Ferris Wheel, and watch the ice city light up at dusk (~16:00). This season also has a *Frozen*-themed area. Warm up regularly inside the heated halls.
