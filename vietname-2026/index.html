Day by Day

The Complete Itinerary

Plan A: all by bus, home on Dec 20. Plan B, which swaps the last night bus for a flight, is under Transport.

Day 01 Thursday, December 17

🚌 Phnom Penh to Saigon

07:30 Giant Ibis terminal, Street 106 near the night market (30 min before departure). Passport, printed e-visa if needed, some US$ cash.
08:00 – ~14:30 Bus to Saigon. At the Bavet / Moc Bai border you get off for the Cambodian exit stamp; the crew collects passports for Vietnamese entry. Lunch at a roadside stop isn't included (~US$3–4).
~14:30 Arrive at the Giant Ibis office, Pham Ngu Lao, District 1. Grab or walk to your hotel (book in District 1 so everything is walkable). Activate your eSIM; withdraw VND.
17:00 – 21:00 Nguyen Hue Walking Street & Ben Thanh. Walk the pedestrian boulevard from the Opera House towards the river at dusk, then street-food dinner around Ben Thanh Market: cơm tấm, bánh xèo.
📍 Giant Ibis, Phnom Penh 📍 Nguyen Hue Walking Street
Day 02 Friday, December 18

🏛️ Saigon, then night bus to Dalat

Why this order: both museums open at 07:30 and are cooler and quieter early. Ask the hotel for a late check-out (or leave bags at reception) since your bus leaves at night.

07:00 Bánh mì breakfast (Bánh Mì Huỳnh Hoa is the famous one).
07:30 – 10:00 War Remnants Museum. Tanks and aircraft in the yard; the indoor galleries are heavy going. Allow 2–2.5 h.
10:15 – 11:45 Independence Palace, a 700 m walk. The 1960s presidential palace where the war ended on 30 April 1975; don't skip the basement command bunker.
12:00 – 13:15 Lunch: beef phở at Phở Hòa Pasteur.
13:30 – 15:00 Colonial core: Notre-Dame Cathedral (outside), Central Post Office (inside), then iced coffee on Book Street.
15:00 – 18:00 Rest. Shower and repack. Put a jacket, toothbrush and charger in your small bag for the bus.
18:00 – 20:30 Early dinner (bún thịt nướng), then the Futa office or its free shuttle. Check the pickup time on your ticket.
~22:00 Sleeper bus to Dalat (7–8 h, through Bao Loc).
📍 War Remnants Museum 📍 Independence Palace 📍 Central Post Office
Day 03 Saturday, December 19

🌸 Dalat & the Flower Festival opening

Why so early: it's a Saturday and festival opening day, so the centre jams from ~09:00. Do the lake first and spend the middle of the day out on Prenn Pass.

~05:30 Arrive in Dalat. Futa shuttle or Grab to your hotel (booked from Dec 18 so the room is ready). Shower, nap, warm breakfast. It will be ~10–13 °C.
07:30 – 08:30 Xuan Huong Lake. Festival flower displays with no crowds and soft morning light.
08:45 – 10:00 Crazy House (Hang Nga Villa). Opens 08:30; you'll beat the tour groups.
10:15 – 12:00 Robin Hill cable car ➔ Truc Lam Monastery. Ride up before 11:30 (lunch stop until 13:00). 12 minutes over pines and Tuyen Lam Lake; quiet Zen monastery at the top.
12:00 – 13:15 Lunch on Prenn Pass: lẩu gà lá é, chicken hotpot with é leaves.
13:30 – 15:30 Datanla Waterfall & alpine coaster. Ride the coaster down to the falls and back up. Swap: Linh Phuoc Pagoda or the flower villages if the coaster is closed for rain.
15:30 – 17:00 Back to the hotel. Rest, check out, leave bags at reception.
17:30 – 19:00 Night Market dinner: bánh tráng nướng, hot soy milk, strawberries.
19:00 – 21:00 Flower Festival opening, Lam Vien Square. Starts 20:00; arrive by 19:00 for a view. Leave by ~21:15.
21:30 Collect bags; Futa shuttle to the terminal.
~22:30 – 23:00 Sleeper bus to Saigon.
📍 Crazy House 📍 Robin Hill cable car 📍 Datanla Waterfall 📍 Lam Vien Square
Day 04 Sunday, December 20

🏠 Saigon to Phnom Penh

~05:30 – 07:00 Arrive in Saigon. Grab to Pham Ngu Lao (District 1).
07:00 – 09:15 Phở breakfast and coffee near the Giant Ibis office; many cafés there open early. Buy water and snacks for the border.
09:15 Check in at Giant Ibis (30 min before departure). Passport ready for the Moc Bai / Bavet border.
09:45 – ~16:00 Bus to Phnom Penh. If your night bus was badly late, switch to the 15:45 departure (arrives ~22:00).
~16:00 Arrive at Street 106, Phnom Penh. Home!
Don't book the 08:00 Giant Ibis on Dec 20. Night buses from Dalat often arrive late; the 09:45 gives you a 2–3 h buffer.

💡 Money tips
  • Bring US$ cash to change, or withdraw VND at a Saigon ATM on Day 1. Markets, street food and buses want cash.
  • Use Grab in both cities: fixed price shown before you ride, no haggling.
  • Get VND before Dalat's outer sights. Card payment is rare at Datanla and the markets.
  • Keep small notes. Few street vendors can change a 500,000 VND note.

Essentials

Travel Tips & Prep

Entry rules, what to pack for two very different climates, and a few words of Vietnamese.

🛂

Entry to Vietnam

  • Cambodian passport: no visa for a short stay (ASEAN exemption), free. Confirm with the Vietnamese embassy before you go.
  • Other nationalities: single-entry e-visa at evisa.gov.vn, US$25, 3–5 working days. Apply 2–3 weeks ahead.
🧥

What to Pack

  • Warm jacket or fleece: Dalat is 22–24 °C by day but 10–13 °C at night, and most hotels have no heating.
  • Light clothes for Saigon (low 30s °C).
  • Closed shoes for walking and the night bus.
  • Rain shell or umbrella, sunscreen (strong UV at 1,500 m).
🛏️

Sleeper Bus Bag

Keep a small bag with you on board: jacket (the A/C is cold), charger, water, toothbrush, wet wipes. Berths are ~170 cm long. Ask Futa for the free hotel shuttle when you book.

💵

Money & Payments

Vietnam is still cash-first at markets, street food and on buses. ~26,000 VND ≈ US$1. Withdraw at a Saigon ATM on Day 1 and keep 20k / 50k notes handy.

📱

Apps

Grab for rides and food (fixed prices, no haggling). Google Maps offline for both cities. A Vietnam eSIM; Viettel has the best coverage in the highlands.
