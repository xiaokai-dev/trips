# 🇻🇳 Saigon & Dalat Trip Itinerary (Dec 17 – Dec 20, 2026)

Oct 25, 2026 · @Daly Kao

## Trip at a glance

Four days, two night buses, one hotel night in Saigon: Phnom Penh to Saigon by bus on Thu Dec 17, overnight sleeper to Dalat on Fri Dec 18, a full day in Dalat on Sat Dec 19, overnight sleeper back to Saigon, and the bus home to Phnom Penh on Sun Dec 20.

**Route:** Phnom Penh ➔ Ho Chi Minh City (Saigon) ➔ Da Lat ➔ Ho Chi Minh City ➔ Phnom Penh

**Big news for your dates:** the 11th Dalat Flower Festival opens on the evening of **Sat Dec 19, 20:00, at Lam Vien Square**, the exact day you are in Dalat. Expect crowds, higher room rates and buses that sell out, so book early. ([VietnamSpot](https://vietnamspot.ru/en/blog/da-lat-flower-festival))

**Entry to Vietnam**

| Passport | What you need | Cost | Notes |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Cambodian | No visa for a short stay (ASEAN exemption) | Free | Confirm with the Vietnamese embassy before you go |
| Other nationalities | E-visa at evisa.gov.vn, single entry | US$25 | Processing 3–5 working days; apply 2–3 weeks ahead. [Vietnamvisa.com guide](https://www.vietnamvisa.com/guide/vietnam-e-visa.html) |

> **Print your e-visa.** Giant Ibis will not let you board without a printed copy. Their Phnom Penh office prints it for free. ([Giant Ibis](https://online.giantibis.com/routes/phnom-penh-to-ho-chi-minh))

*Planning rate: \~26,000 VND ≈ US$1. Prices marked \* are estimates; check them when you book.*

## Places to visit: priority and suggested time

You get one full day in each city, so drop places from the bottom up: ⭐ **Must** is the reason you're there, 👍 **Recommended** is worth it if you have energy, ➕ **Optional** is first to go.

### Money tips

1. **Bring US$ cash to change,** or withdraw VND at a Saigon ATM on Day 1. Markets, street food and buses want cash.
2. **Use Grab** in both cities: fixed price shown before you ride, no haggling.
3. **Get VND before Dalat's outer sights.** Card payment is rare at Datanla and the markets. ([VietnamSpot](https://vietnamspot.ru/en/blog/da-lat-attractions))
4. **Keep small notes.** Few street vendors can change a 500,000 VND note.

## Detailed day-by-day plan

### Day 1: Thursday, December 17 – Phnom Penh to Saigon

- **07:30:** Arrive at the Giant Ibis terminal, Street 106 near the night market (30 min before departure). Passport, printed e-visa if needed, some US$ cash.
- **08:00 – \~14:30 | Bus to Saigon:** At the Bavet / Moc Bai border you get off for the Cambodian exit stamp; the crew collects passports for Vietnamese entry. Lunch at a roadside stop isn't included (\~US$3–4).
- **\~14:30:** Arrive at the Giant Ibis office on Pham Ngu Lao, District 1. Take a Grab or walk to your hotel (book in District 1 so everything is walkable). Buy a SIM or activate your eSIM; withdraw VND.
- **17:00 – 21:00 | Nguyen Hue Walking Street & Ben Thanh:** Walk the pedestrian boulevard from the Opera House towards the river at dusk, then dinner on street food around Ben Thanh Market.

### Day 2: Friday, December 18 – Saigon, then night bus to Dalat

- *Why this order:* both museums open at 07:30 and are cooler and quieter early. Ask the hotel for a late check-out (or leave bags at reception) since your bus leaves at night.
- **07:00:** Bánh mì breakfast.
- **07:30 – 10:00 | War Remnants Museum:** Tanks and aircraft in the yard; the indoor galleries are heavy. Allow 2–2.5 h.
- **10:15 – 11:45 | Independence Palace:** Walk 700 m. The 1960s presidential palace where the war ended on 30 April 1975; don't skip the basement command bunker.
- **12:00 – 13:15 | Lunch:** Phở nearby.
- **13:30 – 15:00 | Colonial core:** Notre-Dame Cathedral (outside), Central Post Office (inside), then iced coffee on Book Street.
- **15:00 – 18:00 | Rest:** Back to the hotel to shower and repack. Put a jacket, toothbrush and charger in your small bag for the bus.
- **18:00 – 20:30 | Early dinner,** then go to the Futa office or meet the free shuttle (check the pickup time on your ticket).
- **\~22:00 | Sleeper bus to Dalat** (7–8 h, through Bao Loc).

### Day 3: Saturday, December 19 – Dalat & Flower Festival opening

- *Why so early:* it's a Saturday **and** festival opening day, so the centre jams from \~09:00. Do the lake first and spend the middle of the day out on Prenn Pass. ([VietnamSpot](https://vietnamspot.ru/en/blog/da-lat-flower-festival))
- **\~05:30:** Arrive in Dalat; Futa shuttle or Grab to your hotel (booked from Dec 18 so the room is ready). Shower, nap, warm breakfast. It will be \~10–13 °C.
- **07:30 – 08:30 | Xuan Huong Lake:** Festival flower displays with no crowds and soft morning light.
- **08:45 – 10:00 | Crazy House:** Opens 08:30; you'll beat the tour groups.
- **10:15 – 12:00 | Robin Hill cable car ➔ Truc Lam Monastery:** Ride up **before 11:30** (lunch stop until 13:00). 12 minutes over pines and Tuyen Lam Lake; quiet Zen monastery at the top.
- **12:00 – 13:15 | Lunch** on Prenn Pass: chicken hotpot with é leaves.
- **13:30 – 15:30 | Datanla Waterfall & alpine coaster:** Ride the coaster down to the falls and back up. *(Swap: Linh Phuoc Pagoda or the flower villages if the coaster is closed for rain.)*
- **15:30 – 17:00 | Back to the hotel:** Rest, check out, leave bags at reception.
- **17:30 – 19:00 | Night Market dinner:** Bánh tráng nướng, hot soy milk, strawberries.
- **19:00 – 21:00 | Flower Festival opening, Lam Vien Square:** Starts 20:00; arrive by 19:00 for a view. Leave by \~21:15.
- **21:30:** Collect bags; Futa shuttle to the terminal.
- **\~22:30 – 23:00 | Sleeper bus to Saigon.**

### Day 4: Sunday, December 20 – Saigon to Phnom Penh

- **\~05:30 – 07:00:** Arrive in Saigon. Grab to Pham Ngu Lao (District 1).
- **07:00 – 09:15:** Phở breakfast and coffee near the Giant Ibis office; many cafés there open early. Buy water and snacks.
- **09:15:** Check in at Giant Ibis (30 min before departure). Have your passport ready for the Moc Bai / Bavet border.
- **09:45 – \~16:00 | Bus to Phnom Penh.** If your night bus was badly late, switch to the **15:45** departure (arrives \~22:00).
- **\~16:00:** Arrive at Street 106, Phnom Penh. Home!

## Pre-trip checklist

- [ ] **Now:** Check your passport has 6+ months' validity. Non-Cambodian passport → apply for the US$25 e-visa at evisa.gov.vn 2–3 weeks ahead, and print it.
- [ ] **Now:** Book the **Dalat hotel for the night of Dec 18** (free cancellation; near Xuan Huong Lake; ask for a room heater).
- [ ] **Now:** Book the **Saigon hotel for Dec 17** in District 1, with late check-out on Dec 18.
- [ ] **Early November:** Book **Giant Ibis** Phnom Penh ➔ Saigon (Dec 17, 08:00) and Saigon ➔ Phnom Penh (Dec 20, 09:45).
- [ ] **By \~Nov 18:** Book both **Futa sleeper buses** (Dec 18 \~22:00 and Dec 19 \~22:30–23:00), with shuttle pickup.
- [ ] **Mid-November:** Check the published festival programme for Dec 19 (opening time, road closures around Lam Vien Square).
- [ ] **Week before:** Install **Grab** and link a card; buy a Vietnam **eSIM** (Viettel coverage is best in the highlands).
- [ ] **Day before:** Get US$ cash and small notes; download offline Google Maps for Saigon and Dalat.

### What to pack

- **A warm jacket or fleece:** Dalat is 22–24 °C by day but 10–13 °C at night, and the festival is an evening event. Most hotels have no heating. ([VietnamSpot](https://vietnamspot.ru/en/blog/da-lat-flower-festival))
- **Closed shoes** for walking and the night bus.
- **Light clothes for Saigon:** it's hot (low 30s °C by day).
- **Small overnight bag** for the sleeper buses: jacket, charger, water, toothbrush, wet wipes.
- **Rain shell or umbrella**, sunscreen (strong UV at 1,500 m).
